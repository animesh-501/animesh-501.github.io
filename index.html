Based in . MSBA graduate, Cal State East Bay (2026). I turn messy operational data into decisions about stock, suppliers and service — how much to hold, where deliveries break down, and where spend is leaking.

Every project here ends in a decision, not just a model — and two of them say plainly that the fancy approach didn't beat the simple one. The question I always start with: what decision does this need to support?
